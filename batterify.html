Privacy Policy

This application is designed with privacy as a priority. Here is what you should know:

  • No Data Collection: Your data is NOT collected or transferred anywhere. All processing happens strictly on your device.
  • Purpose: The sole purpose of this application is to help you manage background processes and close other applications to save battery.
  • Accessibility Service: The Accessibility Service is used ONLY to automate the "Force Stop" action in the system settings for apps you select. It does not monitor your activity or collect personal information.
  • Open Source: This project is completely open-source. You can verify the source code, audit the permissions, or build the app yourself.
